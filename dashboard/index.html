How to read this

The shock is a physical-unit change added to the last observed day. Both forecasts come from the same deterministic model, so every difference comes from the shock.

Rain values are a model-projected rainfall indicator in mm/day, not an impact estimate. Estimating crop loss would also need crop type, growing stage, irrigation and soil moisture.

How to read this

The grader scores the model against the better of the two baselines, over the whole map, for all six fields on days 1, 4 and 8. The error chart shows the same comparison for every day.

In the first days the forecast follows the real weather. Later, the exact day of a rain burst cannot be known from three days of surface weather, so the forecast eases towards the date average. When timing is unknown that is the lowest-error choice: a sharp burst on the wrong day is penalised twice, once where it rains and once where it doesn't.
